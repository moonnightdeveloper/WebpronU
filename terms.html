Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the refund process take?

If approved, refunds typically appear on your payment method within 5-10 business days. The exact timing depends on your payment provider.

Can I get a partial refund?

We do not offer partial refunds for subscription periods. If you're unsatisfied with our service, please contact support for assistance.

What happens if my refund is denied?

If your refund request is denied, we will provide a detailed explanation. You may appeal the decision by providing additional information to support your case.

Do you offer refunds for annual subscriptions?

Annual subscriptions follow the same refund policy. Refunds are only available for qualifying circumstances within the specified timeframes.
